WeZards NFT Minting & MAGES Token Reward Process

Follow the 8-step lifecycle from wallet connection to receiving automatic verifiable MAGES token rewards.

1

Connect Wallet

MetaMask / Trust / Binance

Users connect MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Binance Web3 Wallet or WalletConnect and switch to BNB Smart Chain.

2

Select NFT Quantity

Quantity Selector

Users choose how many WeZards NFTs they want to mint (1 Free per eligible wallet, or up to 10 Paid NFTs).

3

Mint NFT

On-Chain Confirmation

Users can mint through the available paid (0.001 BNB) or eligible free mint options. They confirm the transaction in their wallet.

4

Receive NFTs

ERC-721 Delivery

After successful minting, NFTs are delivered to the user's wallet and displayed in the My NFTs section.

5

Automatic MAGES Reward

100% NFT Coverage

Every minted NFT is eligible for one random MAGES reward. This includes paid mints, Agentic free mints, team mints and owner airdrops.

6

Generate Random Reward

500 - 1,500 MAGES

The reward contract requests randomness through Chainlink VRF. Each eligible NFT receives between 500 and 1,500 MAGES.

7

Receive MAGES Tokens

ERC-20 Direct Payout

After randomness is fulfilled, the MAGES tokens are automatically transferred to the original minter's wallet.

8

Reward History

On-Chain Audit Log

Users can view their NFT IDs, reward amounts, pending rewards and completed reward transactions on the website.

⚖️Enforced Smart Contract Reward Limits
• Maximum eligible NFTs: 5,000 NFTs.
• One NFT = One random reward. Two NFTs = Two random rewards.
• Each NFT can receive rewards only once.
• Minimum reward: 500 MAGES per NFT | Maximum reward: 1,500 MAGES per NFT.
